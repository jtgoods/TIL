in Python, a stack is basically a one-way read/editable list where you can only operate on the 'top' item in the list.

a 'push' adds to the top of the stack

a 'pop' pulls from the top of the stack (and deletes that item)

pushes are done with list.append(item)

pops are done with list.pop() (no index)

this is LIFO (last in, first out) in action.
